> DrawABox Lesson 1
## ✅ Done

- Lesson 1: Lines
	- Using your arms: I learnt how to use the pivots in my arms so I dont use my wrist to draw but my shoulder and elbows wich helps to draw better smoother and more confident lines than precise but wobly lines.
	- Markmaking: I used what I learned in Using your arms to draw better lines and learned three laws or markmaking:
			1. Draw consistent lines, not chicken scratch nor separated.
			2. Draw smooth lines, not wobly.
			3. Draw lines consistent in direction, dont zigzag but start a new stroke.
	- Superimposed Lines: Firts exercise about following a line eight times first made with a ruler, this helped me notice how I fray the lines and how I didnt really had any forethought of where to put my pen.

## 😖 Struggles

- None, it was fun and easy to learn, though I will have to improve my markmaking and confidence.
